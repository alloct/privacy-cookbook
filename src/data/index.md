Data you store is data that can be stolen, seized, leaked, or subpoenaed —
years after you forgot it existed. This section covers the storage side of
privacy: encrypting what you keep, backing it up so loss and ransomware are
non-events, understanding what your files silently say about you, and the
most underrated control of all — keeping less.
