Operational security is the discipline of not defeating your own tools.
Encryption, Tor, and hardened devices fail open when the person using them
reuses a username, mentions their hometown, or answers a well-crafted phone
call. Historically, that is how nearly everyone gets caught — the
[Darwin Awards](/darwin-awards/) section is the case-study companion to
this one.

OPSEC is also the most transferable skill on this site: the same thinking
protects an activist, a business negotiation, and an ordinary person leaving
an abusive relationship.
