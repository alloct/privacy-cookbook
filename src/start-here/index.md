Most privacy advice fails because it starts with tools. This section starts
with the questions that make tools useful: what are you protecting, from whom,
and at what cost to your daily life?

Read these four pages in order. They take about twenty minutes total and will
save you from buying things you do not need and breaking workflows that were
never at risk.
