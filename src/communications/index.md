Communication privacy has two layers: the content of what you say, and the
record of who you talked to, when, and how often. Modern encryption has
largely solved the first layer for anyone willing to choose the right tools.
The second layer (metadata) is harder, less discussed, and in many
investigations more valuable than content.

These pages cover both, and are blunt about where email in particular cannot
be fixed.
