Your browser is the most surveilled piece of software you use. Nearly every
page you load invites third parties to record the visit, and the browser
itself may report home to its vendor.

The good news: browser tracking is one of the most fixable problems in
consumer privacy. Two decisions — which browser, and which settings — remove
the bulk of it.
