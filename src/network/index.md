Every connection you make crosses networks run by someone else: your ISP,
your employer, a coffee shop, a mobile carrier. Each operator sees something,
and the tools in this section change *who* sees *what* — they never reduce it
to nobody-sees-nothing.

That framing matters, because most network privacy tools are sold as
invisibility and are actually trust relocation. These pages spell out exactly
what moves where.
