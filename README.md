# The Privacy Cookbook

A practical field guide to digital privacy and operational security:
threat modeling, hardening guides, criteria-driven tool recommendations,
and a collection of real, sourced OPSEC failures
(the [Darwin Awards](src/darwin-awards/)).

**Live site:** https://theprivacycookbook.com

Inspired by the approach of [Privacy Guides](https://www.privacyguides.org/),
but independent and unaffiliated.

## What the site stands for

- **Threat models before tools.** Every guide starts with who the advice
  protects and from whom. Advice without a threat model is superstition.
- **Practice what we preach.** No analytics, no cookies, no ads, no
  affiliate links, no third-party requests of any kind. Two small
  first-party scripts (theme toggle, and search on the search page only);
  everything else works with JavaScript disabled.
- **Honest claims.** No page on this site says a tool "makes you
  anonymous" or "makes you secure." Recommendations list drawbacks, and
  the [site privacy page](src/about/site-privacy.md) documents what the
  host necessarily processes instead of pretending nothing is.
- **Sourced.** Important claims cite standards, audits, court records, or
  primary documentation. The Darwin Awards section uses the public record
  only.
